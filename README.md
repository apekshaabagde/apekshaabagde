## 🚀 Currently Working on!

### 🤖 [Aasan — Local Service Marketplace & GenAI Connector](#)

Aasan is a comprehensive platform built to bridge the gap between homeowners and local service professionals. It operates on two fronts: as a direct-to-consumer app, it allows users to report household problems using voice, photos, or text to quickly find trusted specialists. It also functions as a B2B GenAI Connector, enabling other platforms to convert unstructured customer complaints into organized work orders and automatically route them to nearby mechanics or electricians. Ultimately, this project highlights my ability to leverage generative AI to solve real-world logistical challenges and streamline complex service workflows.
- **Tech:** `Python` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `Vector Databases` `Generative AI`
